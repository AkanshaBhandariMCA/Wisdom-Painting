Residential

Wisdom Painting has earned a reputation for delivering dependable residential painting services with clean workmanship and long-lasting finishes. Our crews are trained to protect furniture, floors, and high-touch surfaces while keeping your home tidy throughout the process.

Interiors

We prepare every interior surface carefully and use premium paints suited for each room’s use and lighting conditions. Whether you’re updating one bedroom or your entire house, we provide smooth, consistent finishes.

  • Walls and ceilings (all heights)
  • Doors, trims, and baseboards
  • Cabinets and paneling
  • Stair rails and feature walls
  • Detailed prep and clean-up at project completion

Exteriors

Our exterior services are designed for Canadian weather conditions and include proper preparation for durability and adhesion. We use high-performance coatings for siding, brick, wood, and other exterior surfaces.

  • Stucco, brick, and concrete
  • Wood, vinyl, and composite siding
  • Doors, windows, fascia, and eaves
  • Decks, fences, and railings
  • Garage doors and entry doors
